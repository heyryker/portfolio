Not every design project gets a launch party. A lot of my favorite work lives in a slide deck or a single sheet of paper, where the whole job is making something complicated feel simple.

## Presentations

A good pitch or proposal can make or break a startup or an event. The trick is to balance complex ideas with clear layouts, and to use imagery on purpose so the audience stays with you.

At Impact Suite, “Rethinking Stress” (the deck up top) turned a heavy topic into something an audience could actually follow. Each slide makes one point, with a bold color block or illustration doing the heavy lifting: a cracked brain for stress, a simple timeline from calm to eustress, and buttons that read “Eat” and “Breathe” for the basics.

The sales proposal had a different job: look trustworthy and be easy to customize. It moves from the services to customer reviews to pricing, so a sales rep could drop in the details for each client without redesigning anything.

## One-pagers

Tech companies constantly need to explain a lot in a little space: what a product does, how security works, what’s new. A one-pager or infographic is usually the answer. At Lucid, I designed a series of them.

  <Figure src="/blog/decks-and-one-pagers/google-integrations.webp" width={1700} height={2200} alt="A Lucid one-pager titled “Lucid + Google integrations,” listing Lucid products, the Google apps they connect to and what teams can do with them, with a QR code at the bottom." />

The trick with a one-pager is hierarchy. Most people will read the headline and maybe one section. So the headline has to make the case on its own, and everything below it needs to be easy to scan, with short bullets, clear subheads and a table where a table does the job better than a paragraph.

<Figure src="/blog/decks-and-one-pagers/visual-collaboration-suite.webp" width={2400} height={2612} narrow alt="A dark Lucid one-pager titled “We help teams see and build the future,” introducing the Lucid Visual Collaboration Suite with an illustration of people around a giant canvas and three columns for Lucidchart, Lucidspark and Lucidscale." caption="An overview of the Lucid Visual Collaboration Suite." />

The suite overview flips to a dark background to stand out in a stack of light handouts. One headline, one paragraph, then three color-coded columns, one per product, so you can find yours in a second.

It’s not the flashiest work I do, but it might be the most useful.
