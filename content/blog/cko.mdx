Every year Lucid kicks off the new fiscal year with a Company Kick-Off, or CKO. As part of Lucid’s internal creative team, I art directed the visual theme and the branding system that carried it across physical and digital spaces.

The rally cry was “Strong Past, Strong Future.” The identity had to align teams around the world, get people excited to show up, and set an energetic tone for the year ahead.

## The logo

I started with a bold, bubble-letter “CKO” mark. It’s loud and a little playful, which is the energy a kick-off should have. A vibrant, high-contrast palette and a set of playful graphic elements back it up.
