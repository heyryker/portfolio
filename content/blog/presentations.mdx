Not every design project gets a launch party. A lot of my favorite work lives in a slide deck, where the whole job is making something complicated feel simple.

A good pitch or proposal can make or break a startup or an event. The trick is to balance complex ideas with clear layouts, and to use imagery on purpose so the audience stays with you.

At Impact Suite, “Rethinking Stress” (the deck up top) turned a heavy topic into something an audience could actually follow. Each slide makes one point, with a bold color block or illustration doing the heavy lifting: a cracked brain for stress, a simple timeline from calm to eustress, and buttons that read “Eat” and “Breathe” for the basics.

The sales proposal had a different job: look trustworthy and be easy to customize. It moves from the services to customer reviews to pricing, so a sales rep could drop in the details for each client without redesigning anything.
