## The brief

As part of TRON DAO's internal creative team, I art directed a series of ad videos about the ecosystem's partnerships with MetaMask and zerohash. Each one had to explain a technical integration in a few seconds of a social feed.

I set the look and wrote the boards, then worked with the TRON design team and motion designer Rafael Conce at Sons Design to bring them to life. My job was to keep the work clear and on brand from the first frame to the final handoff.

- Visual systems that bridge TRON's sleek, futuristic look with each partner's identity
- Storyboards that move the viewer from dark, tech-driven corridors into vibrant, stylized landscapes
- Direction on pacing, lighting and logo reveals, tuned for retention in social feeds
- Brand-consistency checks through the final handoff
