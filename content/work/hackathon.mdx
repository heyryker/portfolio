## The brief

Lucid's annual hackathon is a cornerstone event that celebrates creativity, innovation and cross-functional teamwork. Planning a good one takes more than a clever prompt and some snacks. It takes a strong visual identity to pull the whole experience together.

## References

The event planning team brought a trove of visual references, including the surreal architecture of M.C. Escher and the game design of *Monument Valley*. We compiled them on a Lucidspark board and annotated what stood out: impossible staircases, floating pathways and a sense of playful illusion. The message we wanted to drive home was that complex problems have more than one solution. Even when they seem impossible, you may just have to change your perspective.

## A modular system

I started by building an isometric grid as the foundation for everything else. Skewed perspective is tricky, especially for symbols that need to feel dynamic and stay readable, so I stripped my tools down to the simplest building block, an isometric square. From it I built a library of modular, 3D-like forms I could duplicate and remix freely.

<Figure src="/work/hackathon/shot2.webp" width={1856} height={982} alt="A library of isometric building blocks and impossible shapes built from a single isometric square" caption="Every form starts from one isometric square." />

## Two directions

The first direction paired an impossible shape, striking and surreal, with clean modern type. The second used the isometric blocks to build the whole event title as a typographic sculpture. I shared both with the program team, and we talked through what was working and how to push it further.

<Figure src="/work/hackathon/shot3.webp" width={1604} height={1296} narrow alt="Two poster directions side by side: an impossible shape with clean type, and the event title built as an isometric sculpture" caption="Direction A, the impossible shape, and direction B, the typographic sculpture." />

The team chose the custom typographic route. It took careful planning to keep it legible, scalable and cohesive, but it also deepened the story. Arches and staircases connect the letterforms, so the title reads like navigating a complex idea with creativity and structure.
