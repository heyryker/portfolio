## The brief

Lucid's 2025 hackathon needed an identity that said one thing: complex problems get solved by changing perspective.

## References

I started with impossible architecture: M.C. Escher's staircases and the paths of *Monument Valley*. I collected references on a Lucidspark board to find what made each one feel impossible.

<Figure alt="TODO(ryker): the Lucidspark reference board" caption="Escher, Monument Valley and friends, gathered on a Lucidspark board." />

## A modular system

Next came an isometric grid and a modular library of 3D-like forms, all built from a single isometric square.

I explored two routes: a surreal impossible shape, and the event title as a typographic sculpture.
