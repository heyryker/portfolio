## The brief

Lucid wanted a brand film about AI at work that went past the usual promise of doing everything 10x faster. The story compares this moment to the move from steam engines to electric motors. The technology arrived quickly, but it took factories about 30 years to change how they worked. It ends on Lucid as the place where teams map their work so AI has the context it needs.

I worked on it from the first concept through the shoot and handoff.

## Backdrops on an LED stage

We shot on an LED volume instead of a green screen. I created the backdrops in Midjourney, from a wall of video calls to a 19th-century factory floor, and they played on the screen behind the actors. That put each scene in camera, with the light from the backdrop falling on the actors.

## Casting, wardrobe and styling

I cast the actors, picked the wardrobe and styled the whole shoot on set. Each era needed to read in a single shot, from period workwear in the factory to the clean, present-day looks around the laptop.

## Typography and styleframes

I designed the typography and the styleframes that the motion designer built the animation from. The history section borrows from old newspapers, with heavy serif headlines and hand-drawn charts on paper. The present day switches to bold sans-serif type over live action, then to Lucid's diagrams glowing on black.
