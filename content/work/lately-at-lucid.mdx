## The brief

Before 2024, every feature and product update at Lucid went to market on its own, each with its own campaign of some size. That's a lot of small launches competing for the same people's attention.

So Lucid pulled them together into one bigger launch that happens twice a year: Lately @ Lucid. After two trial webinars went well in 2024, we scaled it up for the spring 2025 event, a virtual keynote on April 3 called "A new approach to accelerate work." I designed the identity and the campaign around it, from the first email to the last breakout.

## Anchor assets

The event needed branding people would recognize the next time it came around. I built it from a few pieces: a bold stacked wordmark, an outlined @ that could scale up to fill a background, and a ticker strip of repeating LATELY@LUCID text in blue and violet.

Every channel was built from those pieces. Two rules kept it together: one message per piece, and the call to action is always the most obvious thing on the page.

## Email

Email brought in the most registrations. Two invitations went out before the event and one recap after it. A double call to action, with a Register button near the top and another after the agenda, was a main driver. Email registrations went up 55%, to 8,402.

<Figure src="/work/lately-at-lucid/emails.webp" width={2048} height={1317} alt="Three Lately @ Lucid emails side by side: an invitation with the agenda and two Register now buttons, a dark Your work accelerated email, and a post-event email introducing the Agile Accelerator and Cloud Accelerator" caption="Two invitations and the post-event email." />

## Web and in-product

The event got its own registration page, with the event times and the form front and center. A call-out inside the product, paired with a simple banner on the website, sent people there. Registrations from the product and web went up 82%.

## The team

A launch like this takes a lot of people. I worked with product marketing, events, email, demand gen, content, PR and comms, video and motion, learning services and engineering.

Every channel went up, but the result I like best is harder to chart: people started to recognize the event by name. That builds trust with customers and shareholders, because it shows Lucid can build valuable products and explain them well.
