## The brief

SixFifty brought me on to design a gated editorial report showcasing data from a third-party research partner. The goal was to position SixFifty as a thought leader in legal compliance and drive qualified leads through high-value content.

## The approach

I built a clean, editorial layout on a four-column baseline grid that could carry both narrative and data-heavy pages.

- A modular grid for clarity and typographic rhythm
- Custom data visualizations that make complex findings readable at a glance
- Content pacing that guides readers through the insights
- A polished, professional tone aligned with SixFifty's brand
