## The brief

SixFifty brought me on to design a gated report built on data from a third-party research partner. It had two jobs: position SixFifty as a thought leader in legal compliance, and be valuable enough that readers would trade their contact details for it.

## The approach

Research reports tend to read like spreadsheets. I wanted this one to read like a magazine, so I built the layout on a four-column baseline grid that could carry both narrative and data-heavy pages.

- A modular grid for clarity and typographic rhythm
- Custom data visualizations that make each finding readable at a glance
- Pacing that gives every key insight its own moment
- A polished, professional tone aligned with SixFifty's brand
