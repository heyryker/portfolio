## Showing the product without screenshots

Screen recordings date fast and are hard to read at video speed. So the walkthrough shows the product as a simplified version of itself: shapes and lines in Lucid blue, glowing on black, with text reduced to gray bars. You read what the product does, not the words on the screen.

## Isometric layers

The suite is several products working as one, so the main visual idea is layers. Boards stack and tilt into isometric space, and the camera moves through them, the way work moves from a brainstorm in Lucidspark to a diagram in Lucidchart.
