## Part 1: the brand system

I joined TRON DAO as a contract brand designer to help evolve the identity. Right as I stepped in, a miscommunication between the US and China teams led the CN team to launch a micro-rebrand. The result was a piecemeal system that was hard to scale, and the entire US brand operation rested on one internal brand designer. Rather than pause, we used the friction to build real cohesion.

## The audit

The lead US brand designer and I pulled every asset, social post, deck and visual reference from both regions into one FigJam board. Side by side, the problem was clear: individual graphics looked great, but the visual language had no shared rules. We took the board to leadership to show where the inconsistencies were slowing the team down, and together we set a clearer direction that kept the momentum of the recent update while adding structure.

## A living guidelines site

Static PDFs go out of date the moment they're exported, so we built the guidelines as an internal Figma Sites hub we could update in real time. Once leadership and legal signed off, we built componentized Figma libraries: an evergreen set of templates, assets and UI elements ready to use.

## The outcome

The brand went from a fragmented, single-designer bottleneck to an organized, scalable system that gives the US and global teams the tools and autonomy to build consistent TRON DAO work on their own.

## Part 2: the TRON IMPACT design challenge

The prompt: create an imaginary campaign for a sub-brand called TRON IMPACT that brings awareness to the communities, businesses and people TRON helps through everyday stablecoin payments.

TRON's most relevant users are mobile-first (about 68%) millennial men (about 60%), mostly 20 to 30 and middle-income. Many are gig workers or people sending stablecoins like USDT across borders. Everything was designed for a young, on-the-go audience.

I distilled the benefits into three attributes, fast, affordable and reliable, which became the tagline "Stability for Every Wallet" and the line "Lower fees. Faster transfers. Accessible. Anytime. Anywhere."

<Figure alt="TODO(ryker): the TRON IMPACT wordmark and color palette" caption="A wordmark that connects to the main brand as an exponent of success, in red, black and white." />

I explored surreal photography first, but it was too abstract for most users. Wide-angle portraits paired with physical wallet shots felt more human. Desaturating the photos brought them into the brand's tone and let the red accents stand out, and halftones, a grid from the TRON logo's geometry and brackets from the IMPACT mark tied it together.
