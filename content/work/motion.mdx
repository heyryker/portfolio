## TRON DAO partner ads

As part of TRON DAO's internal creative team, I art directed a series of ad videos that highlight partnerships and drive engagement, showing how the TRON ecosystem integrates with partners like MetaMask and zerohash.

I worked closely with the TRON design team and motion designer Rafael Conce at Sons Design to turn complex technical milestones into clear, compelling motion.

- Visual systems that bridge TRON's sleek, futuristic look with each partner's identity
- Storyboards that move the viewer from dark, tech-driven corridors into vibrant, stylized landscapes
- Direction on pacing, lighting and logo reveals, tuned for retention in social feeds
- Brand-consistency checks through the final handoff
